Fair questions

You’re skeptical. Good — you should be.

My phone is always on me.

You answer when you can. RingBack catches the ones you physically can’t — under a sink, up on a roof, running a bead, driving between jobs. Research published in Harvard Business Review found the average business takes 42 hours to respond to a new lead, and 23% never respond at all. A homeowner with water on the floor isn’t waiting 42 hours.

I have voicemail. Isn’t that enough?

A homeowner with an urgent problem rarely leaves one — they hang up and dial the next company in the search results. A text lands in seconds, starts a conversation instead of asking them to wait, and keeps them talking to you while your competitors’ phones ring out too.

Is auto-texting people even legal?

Texting back someone who just called you is compliant — if it’s done right: transactional first message, your name on it, instant opt-out, and no marketing drips without written consent. We register your number with the carriers (A2P 10DLC) and keep every template TCPA-safe. The $49 DIY tools leave all of that legal exposure on you.

I don’t miss that many calls.

Nobody logs the calls they miss — that’s the trap. Call-tracking data across the industry shows about 1 in 4 calls to home-services businesses goes unanswered, and small shops book only about a quarter of the calls that do come in. One saved water heater covers months of RingBack; one saved HVAC replacement covers the year.
